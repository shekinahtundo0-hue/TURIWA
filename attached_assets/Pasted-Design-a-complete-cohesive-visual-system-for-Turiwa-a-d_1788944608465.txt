Design a complete, cohesive visual system for Turiwa, a digital chama (community savings group) app for East African users (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda). Every screen must feel warm, trustworthy, and human-made — like a boutique fintech product, not a template.

COLOR SYSTEM
Core palette: Primary Deep Forest Green #1B5E3A (primary buttons, headers, key numbers, logo). Secondary Fresh Leaf Green #4CAF7D (secondary buttons, links, active states, progress). Accent Soft Mint #E8F5EE (card backgrounds, section fills, chips). Base Pure White #FFFFFF (main backgrounds, input fields). Neutral Off-White #FAFAFA (alternate section backgrounds, dividers). Highlight Warm Gold #D4AF37 used sparsely — balance highlights, premium badges, payout amounts only. Alert Terracotta Red #D9534F for errors only, softened, never harsh pure red. Rules: 60% white/off-white, 30% greens, 10% gold and everything else. Green gradient allowed in exactly two places: the main balance card and the primary CTA button (#1B5E3A to #4CAF7D, 135 degrees). No green-on-green text; always check contrast (WCAG AA minimum). Dark mode: deep charcoal-green base #0E1F16, surfaces #1A2E22, primary green brightened to #5CC98E.

SPLASH / LOADING SCREEN
Layout: background is a soft radial gradient from white center to faint mint edges (#FFFFFF to #E8F5EE). Center: Turiwa logo — a stylized plant whose leaves curve outward to form a circle, suggesting hands joined in a circle (community + growth). Flat vector, two greens plus a single gold dot at the core. Under logo: wordmark "Turiwa" in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold, forest green, generous letter-spacing. Under wordmark: tagline in regular weight, leaf green: "Ushirika. Ukuaji. Umoja." / "Together We Grow". Loading behavior: below the tagline a thin 2px progress line fills left to right in leaf green over ~1.8 seconds — no spinner. Micro-animation: as the bar fills, the logo's leaves gently unfurl and scale from 95% to 100% (subtle, 1.5s ease-out). No percentage numbers, no "Loading..." text. After load: logo scales up slightly and fades, cross-fading into the Welcome/Sign In screen — no hard cut. States: cold start (fresh install) shows full splash with logo + tagline; warm start (returning user) skips tagline and shows only logo + progress line, faster at 0.8s; first frame after splash is skeleton screens matching the dashboard layout (grey-mint shimmer blocks), never a blank white screen.

ICON SYSTEM (anti-AI look — critical)
Style: 2px consistent stroke, rounded line icons — Feather/Lucide family as the base. Rounded line caps and joins everywhere; never sharp miter corners. 24px base grid, optical corrections hand-tuned — a "circle" icon may be 23.5px to look right, embrace these imperfections. Flat only — no gradients inside icons, no shadows, no glows, no 3D. Filled variants exist ONLY for active tab bar states (same silhouette, filled with primary green). Custom icon set to draw (do not pull mismatched stock): Chama — three overlapping people in a circle, not the generic users icon. Contribute — hand holding a coin with a small leaf sprouting from it. Wallet — wallet with a leaf folded into the flap. Payout cycle — two curved arrows forming a circle with a coin at center. Calendar-check — calendar with a single bold checkmark. Shield-verify — shield with a check for KYC/trust states. Megaphone — announcements, single-line strokes. Statement — document with folded corner, lines, tiny coin watermark. Invite — person with a plus badge. Anti-AI checklist: every icon tested at 16px and simplified if it muddies; identical stroke weight across the whole set, no icon heavier than another; no generic sparkle/star/robot motifs anywhere; icons optically centered in buttons, adjusted by eye, not mathematically centered.

AUTHENTICATION EXPERIENCE
Welcome/Gate Screen: top 40% is a mint-green gradient panel with the larger Turiwa logo illustration positioned slightly above center. Bottom 60% is a white rounded-top card (24px top corners) sliding over the gradient. Two CTAs stacked with 16px gap: "Sign In" filled forest green full-width 12px radius, and "Create Account" outlined leaf green same dimensions. Below: "New to chamas? See how Turiwa works →" in small grey-green text.

Sign Up — 3 steps with a segmented progress bar (3 mint segments filling green). Step 1 Phone: large input with country code dropdown (+254 Kenya default; +255 Tanzania, +256 Uganda), auto-formatted as the user types (0712 345 678). Green Continue CTA. OTP: 4 separate boxes, auto-advance, SMS autofill, 30s resend timer. Wrong OTP: boxes pulse terracotta, text "Kanuni si sahihi. Jaribu tena." Step 2 Details: full name, optional email, password with live strength meter (mint to leaf to forest as strength grows), password requirements shown as a tiny checklist that ticks green as satisfied. Step 3 Profile: circular photo picker (camera badge icon bottom-right), skippable. Language toggle English/Kiswahili as a segmented control with leaf green active. Completion moment: "Tumekaribisha! Karibu Turiwa 🌱" with a brief leaf animation.

Sign In Screen: white background, logo top center ~64px. Phone number + password fields: 12px radius, mint fill at rest, white with green border on focus, eye toggle for password. "Forgot Password?" in leaf green, reset flow via SMS OTP not email. Primary CTA "Sign In" full-width forest green with subtle pressed state (darker green, scale 0.98). Divider "or" in grey, then "Continue with M-Pesa" button in M-Pesa green with white text for passwordless push login, plus Google and Apple as outlined white buttons with monochrome brand icons. Footer: "Don't have an account? Sign Up" with Sign Up in leaf green bold.

Post-login touches: success state is an animated green checkmark drawing itself plus "Karibu tena, [Name]!" for 300ms before routing to dashboard. First successful login triggers a biometric opt-in sheet: "Enable Face ID for faster sign in?" with Enable (green) and Maybe Later (grey). Unverified number shows a non-blocking banner on dashboard: "Verify your number to unlock withdrawals →".

Edge states — design all of these: wrong password makes the field shake 4px horizontally with helper text in softened terracotta "Nenosiri si sahihi". Loading swaps button contents to a white pulsing dot trio on green — never a full-screen spinner. Offline shows a slim top banner, mint background, forest green text: "Hakuna mtandao. Jaribu tena unaporudi online." Rate-limited OTP shows a countdown timer replacing the resend button, calm copy, no alarmist red.

TYPOGRAPHY, SHAPE AND MICRO-COPY
Type: Plus Jakarta Sans primary (headings SemiBold/Bold, body Regular, money numbers in Bold with tabular figures), Inter as fallback. Scale: 28 / 22 / 17 / 15 / 13 / 11. Generous line-height (1.4 body, 1.2 headings). Money always bold, forest green, tabular numerals, currency prefix (KSh / TSh / USh) in lighter weight. Shape language: cards 16px radius with 1px #E8F5EE border OR soft shadow (0 2px 12px rgba(27,94,58,0.06)) — never both heavy border and shadow. Buttons and inputs 12px radius. Full-screen sheets 24px top corners. Consistent 16px screen padding, 12px internal card padding. Micro-copy voice: warm, communal, lightly Swahili-flavored ("Karibu", "Harambee", "Pamoja") — never corporate. Empty states: illustrated plus "Hakuna chochote bado — jihusishe!" instead of "No data". Errors blame the situation, never the user: "Something interrupted us. Try again." not "You entered invalid data". Success states celebrate: confetti in greens plus gold, 300ms, tasteful.

Deliverables: splash screen with both cold/warm start states, welcome/gate screen, full sign up flow (all 3 steps plus OTP), full sign in screen, forgot password flow, biometric opt-in sheet, all error/loading/offline states, the complete custom icon set, and a dark mode color sheet — all as high-fidelity mobile screens in one cohesive design system.
