## 2. What the pilot is for

Five groups onboarded and three full contribution cycles, per the strategy document. The
build cannot onboard five groups by itself today (phone sign-up needs an SMS sender, §4 of
the audit), so the pilot's real question is narrower and more useful:

> **Does a treasurer who records a meeting in Turiwa come back for the next one, and does
> anybody in that group argue about the numbers less than they used to?**

Everything in §5 measures that and nothing else.

### Objection bank, prepared from what the code actually does

Answer with the truth and a date, never a maybe.

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "It says 8,100 but the box has less" | "Correct — a loan out is not in that figure yet. That is the first thing we are fixing, and until then the group's cash is the balance *minus* what members still owe." |
| "What about last month?" | "We cannot show it yet; entries are not dated on screen. This pilot is one cycle, and the report is the next build." |
| "Can my secretary have it too?" | "Not yet — one account records. Two logins into one group is what we build after the pilot." |
| "Does it talk to M-Pesa?" | "No. Everything in it is what someone typed. That is exactly why we want your book next to it for a month." |
| "Is my money safe in there?" | "It is a record, not a wallet — no money moves through this app. And we back it up every session; ask to see the backup." |
| "Why should I pay 500 bob?" | "Only you can answer that: what is it worth to not argue about the book at every meeting? If that is less than 500, don't pay it." |
| "The joining fee has nowhere to go" | "True, and it's a one-day fix. Record it as a contribution and note it in the paper book for now." |

## 6. Decision rule at the end (Mon 23 Nov)

Write one of these three, and act on it:

- **Build on.** ≥1 group recorded 3 of 4 weeks, discrepancies were 0 or every one was
  explained, and ≥1 group named a price they would pay and who signs it off. → Priority #1
  (loans in the ledger, task #68) next, then month-end (#69), then the paid ask.
- **Fix then re-run.** Groups recorded but the book won on discrepancies, or month-end was
  impossible. → The pilot found the real blocker: ship #69 (dates/period totals) and #68 and
  run two more weeks. Do not start #70 yet.
- **Stop and re-ask.** Nobody recorded twice without being reminded, or the money answer was
  always "not from me". → This is a pricing or a target-customer problem, not a code
  problem. Interview five more treasurers before writing another feature.

Two more lines worth recording while it is fresh: the one sentence a treasurer said that you
would put on the website, and the one thing you would refuse to build.

## 7. What must be true before any group's real money is on this

From the audit, non-negotiable, in this order: a hosted Postgres with RLS (task #28), the
database backup scheduled rather than remembered (Day 1), 2FA either enforced at sign-in or
removed from the screens (Day 2 §4), and `SHOW_SMS_CODES` off anywhere a real person signs
up. A four-week pilot on one record-keeper's phone is inside what today's build honestly
supports; a group that puts its actual balances in and tells ten other groups is not.
